# Shipping

Express shipping has a flat cost of 9 USD and arrives in one to two business days. Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 USD and takes three to five business days.

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as the courier collects your parcel.

International orders take up to ten business days. Customs duties and import taxes are charged by the destination country and paid by the recipient on delivery.
